On the App Store

xiComic

Comic & Manga Reader

A privacy-first reader for the comic and manga files you already have. No storefront, no ads, no account needed.

Free on the App Store for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision, with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

What xiComic does

  • Open the files you have

    CBZ, CBR, ZIP and RAR files, RAR5 included, from Files, AirDrop, the Share Sheet or drag and drop, plus folders of JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC and GIF images.

  • Read your way

    Comic Mode left to right, Manga Mode right to left, and Vertical Scroll for webtoons and long-strip comics.

  • Organise your library

    Create collections, sort your library however you like, or tap Magic Organizer to group series automatically.

  • Private and offline

    Your library lives on your device and needs no internet connection. Your files never leave your device unless you choose to.
