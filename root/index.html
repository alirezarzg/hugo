xiComic

Comic & Manga Reader

A privacy-first reader for the comic and manga files you already have. No storefront, no ads, no account needed.

Free on the App Store for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision, with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

What xiComic does

  • Open the files you have

    Open CBZ, CBR, ZIP or RAR files, RAR5 included, from Files, AirDrop, the Share Sheet or drag and drop, and import whole folders in one tap. Folders of JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC and GIF images work too.

  • Read your way

    Comic Mode reads left to right, Manga Mode right to left, and Vertical Scroll top to bottom for webtoons and long-strip comics. Pinch to zoom on any page, or tap to hide all controls.

  • Organise your library

    Create collections and sort your library however you like, or tap Magic Organizer to group series automatically. Browse a grid of edge-to-edge covers and search your library.

  • Private and offline

    Your comic library lives on your device and needs no internet connection. Your files never leave your device unless you choose to. Anonymous analytics help improve the app and are never linked to you.

  • Accessible and multilingual

    VoiceOver labels for navigation and reading, and full translations in English, German, Spanish, French and Italian.
